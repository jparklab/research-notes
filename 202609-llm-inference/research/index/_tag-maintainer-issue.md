The author states this is a real mechanism, not a synthetic corner case: 'in 51 minutes of healthy-plus-burst operation, 0 of 8545 requests waited [5 minutes]... The 5-minute waits were then manufactured by the gate's own burst-overshoot behavior.' This is the single most detailed, numerically load-bearing evidence in the corpus of llm-d's cache-affinity routing catastrophically concentrating load onto specific replicas under sustained/bursty conditions, though the trigger is a specific flow-control accounting bug (fixed in this issue) rather than a fundamental property of affinity routing itself. `evergreen`
