Proposes a post-training, weight-only low-bit (INT4/INT3) quantization method for LLMs that requires no backpropagation or reconstruction. Key insight: roughly 0.1-1% of weight channels are "salient" and disproportionately affect output quality; salience is identified by activation magnitude (not weight magnitude), and AWQ protects salient channels by per-channel scaling (scale weights up, scale corresponding activations down by the same factor) before quantizing everything uniformly to INT4/INT3 -- avoiding the hardware-inefficient mixed-precision kernels that a naive "keep salient weights in FP16" approach would require. On a multimodal benchmark (OpenFlamingo-9B on COCO captioning, 32-shot), quantization-induced degradation drops from 4.57 (baseline PTQ approaches) to 1.17 under INT4-g128 -- "4x model size reduction with negligible performance loss" is the paper's own characterization, not an independent audit.
