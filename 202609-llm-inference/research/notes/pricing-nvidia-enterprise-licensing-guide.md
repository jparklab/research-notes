## NVIDIA Education Pricing Program[#](https://docs.nvidia.com/ai-enterprise/planning-resource/licensing-guide/latest/pricing.html#nvidia-education-pricing-program "Link to this heading")
The NVIDIA Education Pricing Program supports the use of AI computing in teaching and research institutions. The program makes it easy to procure and administer NVIDIA solutions, software licensing, and services for qualified educational institutions and helps reduce their total cost. For more information on eligibility, review the [NVIDIA Education Pricing Program](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1Yy8QCIytNEtZjqI703Lxfbkar-LNDCcE) documentation.
## NVIDIA Inception Pricing Program[#](https://docs.nvidia.com/ai-enterprise/planning-resource/licensing-guide/latest/pricing.html#nvidia-inception-pricing-program "Link to this heading")
The NVIDIA Inception Program supports cutting-edge startups in AI, data science and HPC startups with go-to-market support, expertise and technology. The program makes it easy to procure and administer NVIDIA solutions, software licensing, and services for qualified startups and helps reduce their total cost. NVIDIA Inception Members can purchase up to 64 1-year subscriptions of NVIDIA AI Enterprise at a reduced price within a 12-month period. For more information on NVIDIA Inception Program eligibility, review the [NVIDIA Inception for Startup](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/) site.
## NVIDIA Connect Pricing Program[#](https://docs.nvidia.com/ai-enterprise/planning-resource/licensing-guide/latest/pricing.html#nvidia-connect-pricing-program "Link to this heading")
The NVIDIA Connect Program supports companies developing publicly available software in AI, data science, HPC, and all areas of accelerated computing by bringing together the NVIDIA technologies, ecosystem, expert guidance, and marketing resources. The program makes it easy to procure and administer NVIDIA solutions, software licensing, and services for qualified members and helps reduce their total cost. NVIDIA Connect Program Members can purchase up to 16 1-year subscriptions of NVIDIA Enterprise at a reduced price within a 12-month period. For more information on NVIDIA Connect Program eligibility, reach out to connectprogram@nvidia.com.
